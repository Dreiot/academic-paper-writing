# Manuscript production separation and verification

Use this reference for the manuscript and substantive passage tasks routed here by [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md#route-to-the-writing-phase). Scale preparation and verification to the requested text and its necessary dependencies. A short passage can require decisions about evidence selection, comparisons, reasoning or conclusion strength. It does not by itself require whole-paper review, a new record or a separate approval stage.

The hard requirement is a traceable separation between producing a candidate and deciding that it satisfies the scientific and authorial requirements. Subagents and an independent reviewer are useful implementations, not universal requirements. Choose the verification design that best fits the task, available tools, evidence access, cost, and risk.

## Keep one authoritative candidate

Identify one manuscript owner and one authoritative candidate. One authorization to complete a task does not require generating all prose in one pass: the owner may draft dependent parts in stages and deliver one integrated result. Contributors submit scoped candidate text rather than independently overwriting the main file or redefining the paper's contribution. The owner must actually edit the returned material for terminology, emphasis, repetition, citation scope and continuity; concatenating individually checked blocks is not completed integration.

Before drafting, decide what readers should learn from the affected text and identify the direct evidence for it. In the existing brief, retain each result, counterexample and technical distinction whose loss would change that answer or how the study is reproduced. For revisions, identify these items in the author-controlled baseline before cutting or merging prose, then decide what belongs in the body, a display or a linked supplement. Keep different measures separate when they answer different questions. Reuse active author decisions, applicable section and venue requirements, and [focused exemplar learning](literature-and-rhetoric.md#learn-how-strong-papers-construct-the-argument). Use the [shared manuscript basis](manuscript-basis.md#keep-one-reusable-local-file) or current context at the task's scale.

Identify one integrated candidate for completion review and check it against all still-effective requirements together, including inherited wording and relevant neighboring passages. Check meaning, information selection and continuity before final length and format compliance. If a late cut or repair changes a comparison, scope or connection, recheck those effects within the same revision before delivery. Apply the existing [final prose audit](anti-overdefense.md#final-prose-audit). A plan, revision note, change summary, paragraph count or successful render cannot establish that the delivered candidate satisfies the request.

For complete-paper delivery, read the whole final manuscript in order, including inherited sections, figures, tables and the human-readable supplement. Check whether the Introduction develops the need, Related Work establishes the comparison, Method explains the proposed design, and results and discussion develop the answer without repeated restarts or misplaced detail. Use the relevant genre's section roles. A bounded substantive change uses its affected reasoning and necessary context. Report material gaps separately from numerical and layout checks.

## Choose a proportionate verification design

Use one or more of these mechanisms according to what materially improves reliability:

- **Independent reviewer or subagent:** preferred for a complete paper, broad novelty positioning, high-stakes scientific interpretation, or a demonstrated failure of self-review. For a requested comprehensive review, use the perspective of a reviewer for the target journal and article type, or for the author's specified scholarly standard. Give the reviewer the original author requirements, exact candidate package, and direct evidence before producer assessments. Apply the checks under [Review the scholarly argument](#review-the-scholarly-argument-not-only-its-parts) across the requested manuscript. Compare applicable venue requirements with the article's actual scope and distinguish them from editorial judgment. Return actionable, source-located findings by importance and report what text, displays, and evidence were inspected. Review of selected items alone cannot establish whole-manuscript acceptance.
- **Specialist delegation:** literature research, local evidence checking, mathematics, figures, or document engineering may be assigned to bounded specialists while one manuscript owner performs integration.
- **Separated verification pass:** when independent review is unavailable or unnecessary, finish the candidate, set aside the production narrative, rebuild the acceptance checklist from the author's original request and direct authorities, then inspect the final candidate source-first rather than validating the writer's intentions.
- **Deterministic or tool-assisted checks:** recompute derived values when authorized, compare manuscript numbers with accepted tables, validate citation identities and source locations, check formula or OOXML structure, and render the final artifact where layout matters.
- **External author or human review:** use when the user requests it or a scientific decision cannot be resolved from available evidence.

If the user chooses Gemini or another external editor for polishing, follow [external-polishing.md](external-polishing.md) for the optional handoff. A returned polished candidate uses the existing source-first checks; owner integration follows the requested task scope. Editing by another model is not itself an independent scientific review.

Do not call a review independent unless a reviewer that did not participate in drafting inspected the exact candidate and direct evidence. Lack of an independent reviewer does not by itself prevent completion unless the user explicitly requires one or unresolved risk makes the scientific result non-credible; report the verification mode accurately.

## Protect scholarly work in compound tasks

For a compound manuscript task, respect four dependency phases: establish literature and scientific evidence; build and verify the scholarly argument and prose; produce formulas, figures, tables, supplements, and document layout from that stable content; then inspect the integrated final artifact. This is a dependency order, not a requirement to run every activity serially or create separate deliverables. Independent work may overlap, but technical production must not substitute for semantic acceptance or consume an unresolved claim as if it were final. If later evidence changes the method, interpretation, or claim, reopen the affected prose and its downstream artifacts.

Within the writing phase, partition work by content dependencies and subsection responsibilities, not chapter numbers or fixed word, paragraph, chapter or agent counts. From the question, author request and sources, distinguish independently treatable work from passages that need joint editing. Establish the roles of related parts, draft them in dependency order, and read the necessary neighboring context while each is edited. Coordinate repeated explanations, transitions and depth; reopen a stable part only when a relevant change affects it. The owner then edits the parts into one continuous candidate.

For example, a Related Work ending and Method opening need joint treatment; the Abstract, contributions and Conclusion must answer the same question but need not be drafted simultaneously; independent source checks may overlap, while final novelty positioning stays with the owner. These are dependency examples, not a fixed writing order. Do not make blocks approval gates or ask for renewed permission to continue each one. Pause only the affected work when a core-method, evidence-boundary or material-claim decision genuinely exceeds the existing authorization.

Match verification to what changed: language edits need meaning and citation fidelity checks; paragraph reorganization needs argument and neighboring-continuity checks; new interpretations need direct supporting evidence; complete-manuscript delivery needs the integrated check below. Reuse valid verification for unchanged support. These checks do not trigger wholesale literature or research review of untouched sections. After owner integration, inspect the final candidate for correspondence among question, contribution, method and conclusion; consistent terms, symbols and evaluation objects; repeated method introductions or missing explanations; separation of literature facts, study-specific choices and inferences; and changes to another part's claim meaning. A local pass does not replace this integrated check. Conversely, integration does not mean rerunning all experiments, re-accepting established research results or independently reviewing every block again. Recheck the affected parts using the existing A/B semantic criteria below and stop when the requested outcome and necessary checks are satisfied. Intermediate blocks and execution records remain outside the delivered paper.

## Use explicit responsibility contracts when delegating

Use delegation when the work is separable and available capabilities offer a concrete benefit. Favor independent literature checks, formula or number verification, local analysis and bounded drafting; do not default to one author per chapter. When delegating prose, use the existing responsibility contract to give each contributor:

1. the whole-paper question, exact requested outcome, the part's specific job and active author instructions;
2. the authoritative baseline/candidate, necessary preceding and following context, and confirmed terminology;
3. permitted direct sources and study records, with exact paths or locations;
4. its owned scope, editable files and interfaces with neighboring work; the main candidate remains owner-controlled;
5. protected facts and claim boundaries, plus a short author-approved style sample if one exists; style is not scientific evidence;
6. candidate prose or findings for owner integration, source locations, unresolved issues and the required acceptance evidence.

Run independent contributions in parallel only when their inputs and write ownership permit it. These roles are optional, not a required team. Without subagents, the same owner can execute the stages and use the existing separated verification pass; this neither blocks completion nor constitutes independent review.

Require consequential factual outputs to identify direct support and distinguish observations, inferences, conflicts and missing evidence; use status labels only when helpful. The owner must inspect sources and reconcile contradictions, not accept a contributor's confidence or a majority vote as evidence.

## Verify high-impact facts completely

For affected content and its integration dependencies, check every numerical, statistical, novelty/priority, method-characterization, comparative, causal, clinical, policy, and current-platform claim against its direct authority; reuse still-valid verification elsewhere. Verify that every citation supports the exact sentence to which it is attached. Derived values must either be recomputed from authorized data or matched to an accepted result whose derivation and scope are known.

Read the delivered text against the evidence in both directions. For each central claim, locate its exact supporting comparison and check that the verb, population, comparator, measure and conditions still match, including in the abstract, discussion and conclusion where they restate it. For each essential item in the brief or baseline, locate where the reader can recover it in the final body, display or linked supplement. A related measure or a result from another comparison cannot replace it. Restore consequential omissions, and compare wording that defines an operation against the direct method record. Check changes to quantities, signs, transforms, denominators, ordering, eligibility and terms with technical meanings where applicable.

Distinguish the observed result, the inference the comparison supports, and the mechanism or applicability it can establish. Check whether a sentence about a difference across conditions or a cause is supported by the comparison that would test it. If the record supports separate estimates only, report their direction and relevant uncertainty without upgrading them to a finding about their difference. Repair the claim itself where it recurs. Use the [prose audit](anti-overdefense.md#final-prose-audit) to remove redundant caveats after the meaning is accurate.

When evidence needed for the active core question, specific claim or explicit deliverable is insufficient, first distinguish unused existing material, analysis or plotting of existing data, and genuinely new experiments. Reuse valid results and investigate the relevant accessible records; an access failure does not establish an experimental gap. Complete already-authorized analysis or plotting directly. For an unresolved gap, use the existing [authorization and Claim Decision rules](../SKILL.md#escalate-evidence-claim-conflicts-to-the-user) to give the user or Research Controller a compact, source-located recommendation: affected claim, missing support and consequence, smallest supplement and the question it can answer. Estimate material experiment costs only from an available basis. Correcting an overstatement may suffice; do not expand work merely to obtain significance, add figures or satisfy hypothetical reviewers, silently replace the core contribution, or treat added disclaimers as closure of an unmet research requirement.

Research beyond current authorization returns to the user/Controller, not a writer-issued Goal, method/evaluation change or adoption of new conclusions. Follow the [existing governed/standalone boundary](../SKILL.md#work-within-the-authorized-evidence): continue an established codex-research-workflow process where applicable, without automatically invoking or installing it for standalone writing. Pause only dependent content, continue supported writing, and keep the recommendation in the task note. This evidence check adds no Gate, approval or review stage.

## Review the scholarly argument, not only its parts

Whether review is independent or performed as a separated evidence-based pass, inspect at least:

- whether the research problem, prior knowledge, unresolved gap, method response, evidence, and conclusion form a credible journal argument;
- whether literature coverage and synthesis meet [literature-and-rhetoric.md](literature-and-rhetoric.md);
- whether other work is represented fairly and every material claim has direct support;
- whether contributions are distinct, evidence-linked, and no broader than the manuscript establishes;
- whether explanations and interpretations belong in the correct sections and do not merely restate prompt bullets;
- whether the actual text meets the [shared prose standard](anti-overdefense.md): clear supported judgments, connected reasoning, legible sentence jobs, progressive paragraphs, and functionally placed qualifications;
- whether active author or reviewer requests are visibly implemented in the candidate;
- whether the target journal's relevant scientific and rhetorical conventions are met;
- whether the delivered artifact preserves the reviewed content.
- for affected figures and tables, whether substantive numbered body references explain the relevant comparison or relationship, reading conditions, observations and supported interpretation under [drafting.md](drafting.md#explain-figures-and-tables-in-the-main-text); titles or early forward references alone cannot do this work.
- for newly produced or substantially revised visuals, whether scientific accuracy, technical explanation and professional visual quality are jointly satisfied at the intended manuscript size under [scientific-figures.md](scientific-figures.md#treat-visual-quality-as-part-of-scientific-communication); no single layout or decorative style is a universal acceptance criterion.

Classify findings as:

- **Blocker:** the section or manuscript cannot be considered scientifically credible or factually safe;
- **Major:** the requested scholarly outcome, literature grounding, core logic, or claim boundary is not yet satisfied;
- **Minor:** a localized improvement that does not overturn the section's scientific function or main conclusion.

Paragraph counts, keywords, citation totals, textual difference, schema checks, DOCX/Markdown agreement, and successful rendering may support artifact validation but cannot close a Blocker or Major semantic finding.

## Test what the reader can recover

Within the existing source-first or independent verification pass, set aside the writer's change note and completion assessment. Use the author's request, sources and actual output to distinguish two completion levels:

- **A — coherent and basically accurate:** the reader can recover what was done, the main facts and the connections between sections without a material error.
- **B — motivation and research position established:** the reader can also explain why the study remains worth undertaking after prior research, what specifically it adds relative to the nearest work, and how the method and evidence answer that question. Passing A cannot close an author's B-level request.

For B, use the field and study/article type in the manuscript basis to inspect the actual comparison and contribution. Ask what the closest studies already establish, what knowledge or capability this study adds, and why it matters under the stated conditions. For methods work, this includes what direct reuse of the closest method would already answer and what would still require research. Distinguish prior facts, chosen conditions, and design-induced problems; do not demand an algorithmic innovation from an empirical or interpretive study. Quoting “we focus on”, “another division of work”, “more direct variables” or “real hardware” alone does not establish significance. If the reasoning is present only in a production note, it is still missing from the manuscript.

In the same source-first pass, compare representative-study prose with the primary passages: does it retain the supported capability or finding and its consequential conditions, distinguish original-author results from local reproduction, and implement an explicitly requested author–method–achievement introduction? Missing results in an excerpt do not establish absence in the paper; check any necessary results passages before accepting a mechanism-only substitute. Apply [literature-and-rhetoric.md](literature-and-rhetoric.md#derive-positioning-from-the-nearest-work) without adding a separate review stage. Author names and citations alone do not satisfy the request.

Then read the affected sequence without the production explanation. Can a reader restate the studies' concrete shared question, consequential differences and supported comparative conclusion? Can the reader identify the present problem before dependent technical details, explain why the route is chosen, and distinguish the method overview from the contribution summary? Inspect repeated setup and method restarts under [Introduction guidance](drafting.md#introduction) and [continuity](author-revision.md#build-continuity-before-polishing-connectives). Fewer paragraphs, added connectives or fluent prose cannot compensate for missing comparison, lost negative evidence or incorrect technical conditions.

Read each central display with its substantive body explanation. Recover its comparison, reading conditions and material differences from similar-looking results, then verify those against direct records. Check what each design holds fixed and changes before combining findings. Follow the author's caption/notes policy and repair missing explanation in the appropriate location during this same pass.

Apply B to motivation and positioning, at the scale of the affected reasoning for a bounded edit and across the paper for complete original-research delivery, including journal adaptation. Use fidelity or format checks for faithful translation alone, language-only edits and pure formatting. Locate any material gap and its needed support; deliver useful in-scope work with the unresolved requirement clear. Use the same verification pass.

## Close the quality gate

For each distinct review finding, record the affected location, evidence or reader-facing consequence, and the disposition in the existing brief or review notes. The manuscript owner checks the finding and proposed repair separately. For authorized revisions, implement each supported in-scope repair and locate it in the final artifact. Give the evidence or author decision when retaining the text, and state the remaining input when a finding is open. Use the existing decision route for consequential unresolved choices. Give repeated instances a shared finding when the same repair resolves them.

For the final authorized revision after independent review, the reviewed working manuscript may be edited and delivered at its existing path. Update affected companion files in the same package as needed. Create a separate version when requested by the author or required by an applicable project rule. Keep the comparison traceable in the existing review notes or version history.

Recheck the repair in the final saved artifact, including its effect on surrounding reasoning and document organization. Return material repairs to the independent reviewer when practical, or perform a focused new source-first pass. For complete-paper work, verify the matching main text, supplement and assets, working cross-references, and requested formats in the delivered package. Successful rendering and unchanged result values support these checks. Close semantic findings against the actual text and evidence.

Do not claim the manuscript or affected major revision complete until:

- no Blocker or Major finding remains open;
- all active author/reviewer requirements have a verified final location;
- all high-impact facts and literature-dependent claims have passed their evidence checks;
- the final delivered artifact, rather than an intermediate source or revision note, was inspected;
- the delivery note accurately identifies whether the result received independent review, specialist checks, separated self-verification, or another review route.

If a Blocker or Major cannot be resolved within the authorized evidence or task scope, deliver the useful work as a provisional draft and state the unresolved item plainly. Do not downgrade it merely to permit a completion claim.
